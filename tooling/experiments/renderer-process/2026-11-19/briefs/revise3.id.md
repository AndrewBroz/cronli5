## Second readings to cure

1. **Seconds with no minute named** (judge 31, 32, 33, the strongest in the file): «setiap jam, setiap detik sampai detik ke-59» reads as every second, always; «setiap jam pada detik ke-30» reads as second 30 of every minute. A sentence never jumps from the hour to the second: the minute is said.
2. **A stretch end that is not the last firing** (judge 22): «setiap 2 jam dari pukul 05.00 sampai 23.00, setiap menit sampai menit ke-29» ends at 23.00 or 23.29. Every end fires and is the last firing.
3. **«pada tahun 2028, lalu setiap 4 tahun»** (judge 49): a whole year every four years, or once every four years after 2028.
4. **«setiap 2 jam, setiap menit sampai menit ke-59»** and the seconds sibling (judge 19, 35): which hours is unsaid where a whole hour is filled, and 0 to 59 is not recognised as "the whole of it" at one reading, so it is taken as every minute, always.
5. **The union** (judge 41): «setiap tanggal ganjil serta setiap Senin sampai Jumat, …, tetapi tanggal 15 hanya jika …» read as an overlap, with too many conditions. Evidence from three other languages this week, each read by three blind judges: an exception or condition beside a union cannot be given one scope by order or by a trailing clause. The orchestrator's call (Claude Fable 5.2; record it with this evidence): **inside a union, an arm is said positively, by the days that fire**, never with «kecuali», «selain» or a trailing «tetapi … hanya jika»; and the union itself is marked so that it cannot be an overlap. Find the Indonesian for both (the sense for the odd dates without the 15th is "odd dates from the 1st to the 13th and from the 17th to the 31st", in whatever form your date rules give pieces).
6. **A second piece that loses its rhythm** (judge 10): «… setiap 7 menit sampai menit ke-21 dan dari menit ke-35 sampai ke-56» lets the second stretch read as every minute.

## Two taste calls by the orchestrator, for you to carry out and record

- **A whole unit filled at its own finest rhythm is said as a length.** Both blind judges, unprompted, wrote «selama satu menit», «selama satu jam», «selama 30 menit pertama setiap jam», and the second judge says «sampai menit ke-59» / «sampai detik ke-59» is the hardest form in the file. The first schedule reader's objection was to «sepanjang menit itu» after «setiap 15 menit» (which minutes?), not to a length. Decision: a whole minute or hour, or a leading stretch of one, in which every finest unit fires, is a length («…, tiap kali setiap detik selama satu menit»; «setiap menit selama 30 menit pertama setiap jam»); a coarser rhythm inside it, or a stretch that does not start at the top, keeps ends that fire. The length is safe there because every unit in it fires, so a count cannot be off by one firing that matters. Record the two reads as evidence and the residual risk.
