# Brief: repair Simplified Chinese (`zh`) where a comparison with its sister language showed a weaker form

cronli5 turns cron patterns into plain-language descriptions, in the words a native speaker would write for someone reading once on a screen. Two Chinese languages ship, written independently on purpose: Simplified (`zh`, Mainland usage) and Traditional (`zh-Hant`, Taiwan by default, with a Hong Kong style). Today they were compared pattern by pattern (887 patterns). Where they differ for a reason that is not regional convention, one of them has usually found a form that serves a reader better, or one has a defect. No wrong schedule was found in either. You repair **Simplified Chinese**.

Work ONLY in the git worktree `REPO/.superpowers/worktrees/zh-repair` (branch `zh-repair`); use that path explicitly for every command; never `cd` to or edit any other checkout. Never use `git stash`, `git reset`, `git checkout -- .` or switch branches; do not push, tag or change the version. Scratch: `SCRATCH/zh-repair`. Edit only `src/lang/zh/`, `test/lang/zh/`, and, at the end, the generated files and the two hand-kept ones named below.

## Read first

1. `CLAUDE.md`, in full. Above all: "What all of this is for"; "The reader's mind" (a person reads once, at the pace of speech, holds about four things; recognises clock times and dates at a glance; works out sums with effort; the four costs of a sentence: spoken length, objects held, repetition, operation tax, which are separate columns and are **never added into one number or used as a target**); "How quality is judged" (model readers favour verbose forms; a wish for more words decides nothing; **a cadence that begins on the first second, minute or hour of its cycle and tiles it is said bare, and readers asking "from when?" do not reopen that**; no bracket shows a series of numbers to illustrate what the words already say); "A language is its artifacts" (a failing row has three allowed responses; rows are hand-written, never produced by running a renderer; bug fixes are test-first); "What a renderer must hold to" (continuity; conditions are about how many values and how arranged, never which; what carries between languages is the concern, never the device).
2. The comparison: `SCRATCH/zh-compare/report.md` (full report; `pairs.json` and `diff.txt` beside it hold every pair). It names each kind of structural difference, gives examples with both sentences and the true schedule, and a verdict.
3. `src/lang/zh/usage.md` (how Simplified Chinese writers really write such things), then `answers.md` (what was chosen, why, on whose reading and when: evidence to weigh and test again, not law; a recorded "readers preferred the longer form" is weak evidence), then the renderer and `test/lang/zh/`.

**The sister language's sentences are evidence of a concern met, not wording to copy.** You may read the Traditional sentences in the report and in `pairs.json`; do not read `src/lang/zh-hant/` (its answers and renderer) at all. A Taiwan or Hong Kong form is not evidence for Mainland usage. For each item, name the concern, then find how Simplified Chinese's own usage notes and writers meet it. If the only cure you can find is the sister's form in other characters, say so and justify it from Simplified Chinese's usage notes or a source, or leave the item and report it.

## What to repair

Simplified is a released language; that is not a reason to leave a weaker form in it. Every kind in the report whose verdict is "(b) S" or "(a) T" is in scope. In order of weight:

1. **Hours on a step are not said bare.** `0 */3 * * *` is 每天0:00起每3小时一次 (Traditional: 每3小時一次), and the report counts four different forms for this one kind (偶数整点, a stated start, a window, a list). The answers record that on 2026-11-14 two model readers read the bare step as counted from when the task starts, and the language was amended to never say hours by a bare step. That is exactly the reader objection the project has since settled against: a cadence that begins on the first hour of the day and tiles it is said bare, in every language (`README.md`, "Description Accuracy"; `CLAUDE.md`, "How quality is judged"). Bring Simplified into line: a tiling cadence of hours from 0 is its step alone, at minute 0 and, by one stated rule, under a minute or a finer rhythm; one form per kind. Keep what is genuinely good and its own (the report prefers Simplified's 奇数整点 for `0 1/2` to the sister's stated start: decide what the odd and even hours are called and use it consistently). A cadence that does not start at 0 or does not tile is not bare.
2. **The bracket （首次9:05，末次17:45）** after an hour range with named minutes (`5,20,45 9-17 * * 1-5`). The project allows no bracket that restates what the words say, and the range before it names ends (9点至17点) that are not the first and last firing. Say the stretch once, by the moments that fire first and last.
3. **A step left to inference** (1日、4日、7日……31日): the ellipsis series is the device `CLAUDE.md` rules out ("states a step in words, and shows a series only to illustrate a step already stated"). Decide what a cadence of dates is in Simplified, at each length, with one reading (the sister lists them and says 每月8日至30日的双数日 for every second date between two dates; the old 每隔 problem recorded in your usage notes still applies).
4. **The smaller kinds** where the report's verdict favours the sister: dates a month lacks and counts back from the month's end (the date itself where it comes to one; a 31st that not every month has); weekday sets that cross the weekend (your own usage notes have a sourced 每周三至次周周一); a date-or-weekday pair when nearly every day fires; hours that hold minutes, from three hours up; month parity (the answers refused 单月 for its ambiguity: is there a form without it?); missing values and wraps (每10分钟一次 with one minute excepted; a run of dates across the month's end); the gloss for the nearest weekday, which should state the direction.
5. **The report's own second readings for Simplified**: the stated starts (cured by item 1), the …… series (item 3), and 每分钟的30秒, weakly heard as a duration.
6. Two items long recorded as open in `tooling/docs/finishing-1.0.md` for Simplified, if the work above touches them: 「9点、17点的0分至29分，每分钟一次，以及45分」 (the one-stretch risk) and 「各一轮，每轮60次」 (read correctly, called awkward). Fix them if you find a form with one reading that a Mainland writer would sign; otherwise leave them and say so.

Where Simplified's form is the better one by the report (奇数整点; 每逢偶数年（2030年除外）; `15,45 0-16/2`), leave it.

For every item: decide as a native editor whether the report is right (say so where it is not, with the fire times or the usage evidence); then amend `answers.md` first (the choice, the reason, the evidence, the rejected alternative, today's date 2026-11-19, and that the lead came from the comparison); then write the intended sentences BY HAND into rows (correct existing rows, with the reason; add hard-case rows for forms not yet pinned), work their fire times by hand, run the suite and watch them fail; then change the renderer by a general rule over the form of the sets. No code path for one example. Keep `lexicon.ts` and `continuity.js` in step; if a fusion changes, declare it by name. Stay within the branch budget.

Before calling a new sentence better than the one it replaces, compare the two: with the four cost columns side by side (by hand, or with `compare` in `tooling/scripts/reading/cost.mjs` if this language has an entry there; if it has none, do it by hand and say so), and by reading both cold. Better means no worse in every column, or a mixed result with the judgment stated. Shorter is not better on its own. Record each comparison in `SCRATCH/zh-repair/old-new.md` (pattern, old, new, columns, verdict).
