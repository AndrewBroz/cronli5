## Consistency (decide each, and make the answers say it precisely)

- **A tiling rhythm is said as a rhythm, bare, wherever it stands:** under «짝수 시» the rows say «0·15·30·45분» in some places and «15분마다» in others, and «정각부터 50분까지 10분마다» where «10분마다» says it all (judge 14, 37, 53, 61, 62, 52). A cadence that starts at the top of its unit and tiles it is the step alone; only an off-beat or non-tiling one carries its ends.
- «0분» in a list of minutes is «정각».
- «시대» only where the whole hour, or a rhythm inside it, is meant; with listed minutes the judge finds it surplus (4, 11, 67). You rejected «…18시의 15분과 45분» because «의» sits on the last hour alone: choose the form with one reading and say why.
- One wording for a whole hour: «한 시간 내내», «…시대에», and «9시부터 9시 59분 59초까지» are three ways (judge 41 is the last one left with explicit seconds).
- «N일 간격으로» directly before a time with nothing between (judge 27, 30, 45, 54; and «간격으로, 그중 …»): the judge would write «N일마다». You moved from «마다» to «간격으로» to avoid "from the 1st to the 5th"; three blind judges have now read both and none took that path. Decide.
- The L-n table forms (judge 6, 17): the day numbers heard as lengths, and «0시» hanging on the last item.
- «…25일까지 4일 간격으로, 윤년에는 29일에도 0시» (judge 40): «0시» hanging on the last clause.
- 오전/오후 carried over a list where «8시대» appears twice.
- «1월·4월·7월·10월» against «15·45분»: the unit once or each time.
