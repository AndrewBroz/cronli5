## Second readings to cure

1. **雙數年** (judge 53): a Taiwan reader may judge odd and even by the 民國 year, and 民國 and 西元 parity are opposite (2026 is 民國115). A real second reading that only a Taiwan reader would find. Your usage notes say where each era is used; the year field is 西元. Name the era where parity of years is said, and check every other place a year stands alone.
2. **每年（6月及2030年除外）每天 09:00** (judge 54): still heard as June 2030. The judge: 「每天 09:00（每年6月、2030年全年除外）」. Whatever the form, the month must be marked as every year's and the year as the whole year.
3. **每15分鐘的0、15、30、45秒** (judge 103): read as every 15 seconds, always; and the whole family 「每15分鐘的＋秒／第1分鐘內」 (judge 1 to 3, 22 to 24, 99, 104, 105) is called the heaviest translationese in the file: Chinese does not say "the 5th second of 15 minutes". The judge names the minutes as positions on the clock face: 「每逢整點、15、30、45分…」. A cadence of minutes that holds seconds inside it is said by the minutes it lands on, while they are few; say what happens when they are many.
4. **第1分鐘內** (judge 100, 101, 104): beside 「1分」 (which is :01) in the same set of sentences, 第1分鐘 can be the :01 minute. The judge: 「整點起的一分鐘內」, and ordinals in Chinese numerals (第一分鐘, 第二個週一) so that an ordinal never looks like a clock number.
5. **Hong Kong: 每天上午8時、中午12時及晚上6時的5、20、45分** (judge 107): the minutes hang on the last hour only. Each hour carries its own day part, so they read as three separate items. With so few moments the clock times themselves are available.
