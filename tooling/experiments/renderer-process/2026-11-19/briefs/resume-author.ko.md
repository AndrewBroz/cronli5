## Why this pass

The artifacts were written on 2026-11-13 and parked after several reads. Three things have happened since, and the artifacts must catch up with all of them before a renderer is built:

1. **Open findings from the last reads were never cured.** They are summarised in the ko row of the table in `tooling/experiments/renderer-process/2026-11-13/STATE.md` and given in full in `tooling/experiments/renderer-process/2026-11-13/reads/ko-*.md` (the highest-numbered files are the latest; `ko-read-N.md` are schedule reads, `ko-N.md` blind fluency reads). `leads.md` beside STATE.md lists the traps that held across languages. 2. **`CLAUDE.md` was rewritten** (read it in full, now, before anything else: "What all of this is for", "The reader's mind", "How quality is judged", "What a renderer must hold to"). In short: the reader is a person reading once at the pace of speech, holding about four things; a description is concise AND natural, with one first reading which is the right one. Model readers favour verbose forms, so earlier passes that added stated starts, windows, glosses or brackets of numbers because "a reader asked for it" are suspect: reopen them. Two conventions are settled project-wide: a cadence that begins on the first second, minute or hour of its cycle and tiles it is said bare (the Korean equivalent of "every 2 hours", "every 15 minutes", with no stated start, no window, no bracket); and no bracket shows a series of numbers to illustrate what the words already say. A step that does not tile its unit ("every 7 minutes") is never said bare: it is said with where it stops and starts again, or as its values. Use the cost model (spoken length, objects held, repetition, operation tax) as evidence when choosing between two wordings that each have one reading.
3. **The core and the test scaffolding moved.** `tooling/docs/core-calendar-facts.md` documents facts the answers should be written over (which months a date reaches, leap-year-only schedules, schedules that never fire, `nearestDayIsLast`, `dateAddsNothing`, both-day-fields-must-match `analyses.day.both`, open ranges, cadence pieces). `tooling/docs/language-questionnaire.md` changed; re-check your answers against every question. Every language's style table now has `separator` (the clock separator) and `short` means "the abbreviations the language conventionally uses, where it uses them" (see `docs/styles.md`). The suites changed shape: for FILE FORMAT only, compare `test/lang/it/paradigms.js`, `continuity.js`, `style.js`, `lexicon.js`, `src/lang/it/status.json` and `src/lang/it/options.ts` with yours and bring yours to the current shape (imports, helpers such as `test/lang/parsed.js`, the style fields). Do not read the body of any other language's `answers.md` or its rows for wording: every device another language uses was decided for that language. A Korean sentence explainable as "what English does, in Korean words" is the failure this process exists to prevent. Do not read any renderer code (`src/lang/*/index.ts` and sibling modules other than `options.ts`/`lexicon.ts` for format).
