## A whole unit filled: also the orchestrator's call

«오전 9시부터 9시 0분 59초까지 1초마다», «각각 0초부터 59초까지 1초마다», «매시 정각부터 0분 59초까지»: exact, and nobody writes «0분 59초». Both blind judges, on first reading, took «1분 동안 매초» / «1분간 매초» to be the sixty seconds of that minute; the end-point doubt was marked weak by the first and not raised by the second, who rewrote every such row to «…부터 1분간 매초». Decision: **a whole minute or whole hour filled at the unit's own finest rhythm is said as the unit's length** («오전 9시부터 1분간 1초마다», «한 시간 내내 1분마다», or your best native wording of the same); **a partial stretch or any coarser rhythm inside it keeps explicit ends that fire** («0초부터 50초까지 10초마다»). The length is safe only when the rhythm is the finest and the unit is whole: the count cannot be off, because every second of the minute fires. Record the evidence (two blind reads) and the residual risk.
