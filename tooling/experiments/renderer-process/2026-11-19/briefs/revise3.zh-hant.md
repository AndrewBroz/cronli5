## Second readings to cure

1. **前1分鐘 / 前1小時** (judge 11, 14, 17; weaker 2, 5, 7): 前1＋unit is ordinarily "the one before" (起飛前1小時), so 每小時的前1分鐘 is xx:59 to some readers. With a larger number (前30分鐘, 前10秒) the judge had no doubt. The judge's own forms: 第1分鐘內, 第1個小時內, 整個小時內, 整點起1分鐘. Also 前11分鐘 for minutes 0 to 10 must be converted and sits beside a stretch written by its ends (judge 23): one way of writing a stretch inside the unit.
2. **A window written to the minute over firings counted in seconds** (judge 11 to 14: 09:00～17:45 followed by seconds): the reader cannot tell whether 17:45:30 or 17:45:59 fires. Every end of a stretch is a moment that fires, written to the precision of what fires.
3. **每月1日起每5天** (judge 54): 每5天 against 每隔5天 (1, 7, 13). The judge says careful readers take 1, 6, 11 and that seven dates listed read better than the rule. Decide where the list ends and the rule begins, by how many dates.
4. **每週四及週日至週二** (judge 59, and 60 more lightly): the reader first takes 週四及週日, then has to rebuild. Order of pieces.
